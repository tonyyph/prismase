Prismase

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Applies to Prismase 1.0.0 for iOS

Prismase is a puzzle game. It needs no account and keeps your game on your device. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only part of the app that sends data off your device.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any personal information. There is no sign-in, no profile and no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, coin balance and settings are saved locally in the app's storage on your device. They never leave it, and are removed when you delete the app or choose Settings → Reset progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Prismase shows full-screen ads between some levels, and optional ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data, for example which ads were shown or tapped, and performance or crash data from the ad SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you deny it, ads still appear but are less personalised.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested you are shown Google's consent form, where you can choose which purposes to allow or refuse.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. For data held by Google, see the Google links above.

Children

Prismase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
