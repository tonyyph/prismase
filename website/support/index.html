Support

Found a bug, stuck on a level, or have an idea? Email tonyphvincent@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number if it is about a level. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a crate to lift its top piece, then tap another crate to set it down. A piece can go on an empty crate or on a piece with the same emblem. Fill every crate with four of a kind to win.

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before it reaches you. If you get stuck, use Undo, the lantern Hint (it always points to a move that can still win), or add an extra crate.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. It is kept when you close or update the app, but it is removed if you delete the app. There is no account or cloud sync.

Why does the game show ads?

Ads keep Prismase free. Full-screen ads appear only from level 6, at most once every few wins. Other ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a hint, a skip or double coins.

How do I turn off personalised ads?

On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking for Prismase. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without a connection; ads simply don't appear.
